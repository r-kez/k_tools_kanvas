# Comparing two images (A/B)

A **comparison card** shows two images from your board side by side, split by a divider. Use it to check a before and after, two versions of a paint-over, or a photo against its reference.

## Creating one

Use **Add > Compare A/B**. The card appears at the pointer, or at the center of the view. It moves, resizes, locks, snaps, and joins frames like any other card.

## Choosing the images

The card has two buttons in its top-right corner, **A** and **B**.

1. Click **A**. The cursor becomes an eyedropper.
2. Click an image on the board. It becomes A.
3. Do the same for **B**.

**Shift+click** clears a side. **Esc** or right-click cancels. A button turns solid once its side has an image. The same pickers are in the **Selection** panel.

The card points at the images, it does not copy them. The originals stay where they are, and changes to them (including brightness, contrast, saturation, and tint) show in the card. The card does not apply an image's mask or Color Markup, and ignores its rotation and shear.

## The divider

A is shown to the left of the divider and B to the right. Set its position with **Divider** in the **Selection** panel. Each image keeps its proportions and is centered in the card, so images of different shapes get empty space around them instead of being stretched.

## Broken references

If you delete an image that a card uses, that side shows **Pick A** or **Pick B** again. **Check Board** lists these cases and clears them. It never deletes anything.
